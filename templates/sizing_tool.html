    What this page gives the architect team

    One place to see what runs today before choosing the replacement.

    • Grouped by device type. Access points, controllers, switches, routers, firewalls and load balancers, each with its models, brands, redundancy and tiers.
    • Every component. Chassis, modules, power supplies, fans, stack cables and optics with part numbers, added up into one bill of materials.
    • Licences and features. The licences and entitlements the captures hold, and the features switched on with how many objects sit behind each (VLANs, BGP peers, policies, virtual servers, WLANs).
    • Today and target. What a replacement must carry now, and with your growth margin, plus the figures a configuration cannot hold (throughput, sessions) listed per device type as a checklist to collect.
    • Honest gaps. Each missing command (no inventory, no licence output, no transceiver table) is listed with the devices it concerns, so the architect knows what to ask for.
