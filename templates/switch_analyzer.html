Physical port speed is derived from each interface's own name where the vendor's naming encodes it (Cisco full names, Huawei GE/10GE/25GE/100GE); Cisco NX-OS (Nexus) and ArubaOS-Switch don't encode speed in the interface name at all, so those ports are reported separately as "speed not in name" rather than guessed.
